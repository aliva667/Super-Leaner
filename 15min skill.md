# The 15-Min Cognition Warp

A scientific framework to deconstruct any complex discipline and master its core worldview within a single human concentration cycle, leveraging peak focus to achieve rapid cognitive alignment.

**Tradeoff:** This framework biases toward core mental models over operational execution. For practical mastery, follow-up practice is required.

## 1. Pre-Input Diagnosis

**Don't start blindly. Surface baseline constraints. Align expectations.**

Before starting the timer, you must collect three data points:
- **Baseline:** Map your existing underlying mental models (e.g., "took calculus 5 years ago", "zero foundation").
- **Motivation:** Explicitly state *why* you are learning (e.g., "for strategic business decisions", "pure curiosity").
- **Targeting:** Categorize your desired mastery level. Be decisive:
  - *Level A (Conversational):* Understand terminology, build social leverage.
  - *Level B (Mental Model):* **[Core Target]** Build discipline intuition to analyze real-world scenarios.
  - *Level C (Operational):* Deriving formulas, writing execution code. 

*Note: If the diagnosis requires Level C, immediately abort. It cannot be done in 15 minutes.*
